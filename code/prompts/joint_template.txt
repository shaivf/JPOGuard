# Task Description
You will receive an image, a news headline, a full news context, a reader's surface interpretation
and event implication for the image-headline pair, a reader's surface interpretation and event
implication for the full news context.

Complete BOTH parts:

**Part 1 — Misleading judgment**
- If a reader forms an impression about the nature, status, cause and effect, the responsible party,
  or severity of a news event when only exposed to the image and headline, and this impression is
  significantly corrected, restricted, or overturned after reading the full news, it is misleading.
- If the full news only elaborates, extends, or supplements the headline's implication without
  altering the reader's basic understanding, it is non-misleading.

**Part 2 — Headline correction (only if misleading)**
- If and only if the pair is misleading, rewrite the headline so that it no longer induces a
  misleading impression while remaining faithful to the full news context.
- The rewritten headline must be factually accurate and neutral in tone.
- The generated news headline may contain at most 3 more words than the original headline.
- Original headline word count: {{ORIGINAL_WORDS}}. Maximum rewritten headline word count: {{MAX_WORDS}}.

Full News Context: {{CONTEXT}}

Reader Interpretations: {{READER_INFER}}

# Output (JSON only)
{
    "Misleading": "Yes/No",
    "Reason": "Not less than 100 words, focus on the event level.",
    "Rewritten_Headline": "the corrected headline if Misleading=Yes, otherwise an empty string"
